## Rocket Lab Electron (original model)

Original Spacebounds model and carbon twill texture. The supplied museum, launch, pad, underside, fairing, exploded-view, and blueprint images served as visual references. Their original URLs were unspecified. [Art direction](https://linear.app/spacebounds/issue/3D-1/art-direction-and-technical-spec-for-all-3d-assets-read-first) and [Electron specifications](https://linear.app/spacebounds/issue/3D-3/launch-vehicle-rocket-lab-electron-launcher-electron)

The 4K Electron livery uses [Rocket Lab's Flight 30 photography](https://rocketlabcorp.com/missions/launches/the-owl-spreads-its-wings/) and [Electron Payload User Guide](https://rocketlabcorp.com/assets/Electron-Payload-User-Guide-7.0-v6.pdf) as placement references. The [Rocket Lab mark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rocket_Lab_logo.svg) is rasterized and recolored from the Wikimedia Commons vector.

## Falcon 9

The 4K Falcon 9 livery uses [SpaceX vehicle photography](https://new.spacex.com/vehicles/falcon-9) and the [Falcon User's Guide](https://www.spacex.com/assets/media/falcon-users-guide-2025-05-09.pdf) as references. The [SpaceX wordmark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SpaceX-Logo.svg) is rasterized from the Wikimedia Commons vector. The fairing and service stencils are generic, so the same vehicle can be used across missions.

## Vega-C (original model)

`assets/3d-models/rockets/vega-c/launcher_vega_c.glb` uses original Spacebounds geometry and livery. The supplied profile illustration, pad photograph, and flight render served as visual references. Their original URLs were unspecified.

## Earth and Kennedy Space Center

- Earth uses [Natural Earth land and lake geometry](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/) and [NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/#august) by Reto Stöckli, with ocean colors adjusted. The regional Florida texture is cropped from [NASA’s August Blue Marble imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/) at 500 meters per pixel. The game export retains the 20 patches and uses a continuous shell below the launch area, with relief shown in the texture.
- Kennedy Space Center LC-39A is an original Spacebounds model with generated textures. Supplied aerial and launch photographs, a pad location map, and an in-game screenshot served as visual references. Their original URLs were unspecified.
