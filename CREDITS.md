## Earth and Kennedy Space Center

- Earth uses [Natural Earth land and lake geometry](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/) and [NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble imagery](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blue_Marble_Next_Generation_%2B_topography_%2B_bathymetry.jpg) by Reto Stöckli, with ocean colors adjusted. The game export retains the 20 patches and reduces the raised relief to 24 meters so the globe sits below the launch area.
- Kennedy Space Center LC-39A is an original Spacebounds model with generated textures. Supplied aerial and launch photographs, a pad location map, and an in-game screenshot served as visual references. Their original URLs were unspecified.
